2026-10-12. Follow-up to [the five-file investigation](research-api36-headerless-framework-source-license.md), within issue #1. This records new immutable upstream history evidence, not distribution clearance. All five pinned files remain unresolved for a file-specific applicable grant.

## What advanced

Direct public Gitiles downloads independently reproduce all five exact pinned blobs and SHA-256 hashes. Both immediate parents contain the same five blobs. A historical merge snapshot reproduces the **exact pinned FullBackupDataOutput blob**, rather than merely a similar class. A historical assist comparison explicitly reports an AssistStructure rename that removes its Apache header; AssistContent is deletion plus addition with substantive changes. WorkSource's verified introduction was already headerless.

These are bounded history anchors. A complete commit ancestry and change chain from each historical anchor to the pinned revision was **not** established. Header removal does not by itself establish relicensing, and an older notice does not establish the grant for every later addition.
