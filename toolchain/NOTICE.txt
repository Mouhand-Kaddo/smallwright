Compiler binaries are ignored local inputs and are not tracked in this repository.
toolchain/manifest.json pins their SHA-256 values, and the build refuses
inputs that do not match.

  SHA-256 69dad18a1fcacd342a7d44c5abf74f50e7529975553a24c64bce0b29b86af497.
  EPL-2.0; source and license text in licenses/ecj/.
- R8 8.13.23 from Google's Maven repository, SHA-256
  e3cdcb003d9beca956209ad6b9e9df31f26b732bfaed9c7c8674e903ca9f3b81.
  Its LICENSE is preserved verbatim in licenses/r8/LICENSE.
- AAPT2 built from source for ARM64, SHA-256
  ed917a383f890b2cad3eb3be00657f20d4fc6f73ca5bbbabf062d357633374e7.
  Build record in docs/validation/aapt2-source-build.md; notices for its
  linked components in licenses/aapt2-source-built/ and licenses/aapt2-aosp/.
  The older lzhiyong/android-sdk-tools 35.0.2 binary is not shipped in
  release builds.

The worker compiles against Google's Android API 36 android.jar. The APKs do
not package it: the controller downloads it from Google during setup and the
worker keeps it unchanged in private storage. Only generated app classes enter
DEX. ECJ also compiles the three unchanged sources of AOSP libcore's
core-lambda-stubs-for-system-modules (libcore commit
de876a01b29230b877c9f408348c38a90ee724a5, ojluni/src/lambda/java/java/lang/invoke/),
which declare the java.lang.invoke classes lambdas need and android.jar lacks.
They are GPLv2 with the Classpath exception; the worker ships them as assets
with their headers, and licenses/core-lambda-stubs/ holds the module's license
text. D8 desugars every use, so none of them enters a generated app.

See licenses/INDEX.md for every bundled input, its license and where the text
is.
